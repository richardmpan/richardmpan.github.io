Yi Pan.

Targeting Molecular Mechanisms in Cancer.

Yi Pan, DVM, PhD, is a biomedical scientist trained in veterinary medicine, translational biosciences, cancer biology, and cardio-oncology. His research spans acute myeloid leukemia, clonal hematopoiesis, and cardiovascular disease, with an emphasis on understanding the cellular biology of leukemia stem cells and systemic biology of how malignant and pre-malignant blood cells interact with the body. His work integrates animal models, human datasets, multi-omics, and computational approaches to identify therapeutic strategies that improve leukemia treatment while reducing organ toxicity. Drawing on his clinical veterinary background and translational research training, Dr. Pan aims to gain a comprehensive understanding of the cancer life cycle and develope more precise approaches for cancer treatment.
